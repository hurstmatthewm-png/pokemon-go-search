Trade list builder (pick regional forms)

Add the Pokémon you want, then tap the form for each, e.g. Hisuian Voltorb but not Kanto Voltorb. Send the list to a friend with “Share list”.

Tip: you can paste a whole list like “hisuian voltorb, alolan vulpix, flamigo”. Pokémon names are used so the game's “Show Evolutionary Line” option still works; a few (like Mew, which would also find Mewtwo, or names with spaces such as Mr. Mime) use Pokédex numbers instead. Use “Open in builder” to add extras like !traded.
Battle League: what's on now

A search for each GO Battle League league and cup that's on right now, built from PvPoke's rankings. Leave the rank boxes empty to include every ranked Pokémon, or type a range (e.g. 1 to 100), then copy.

Tip: to add an IV filter such as Great League candidates, turn on “Add to my search” at the top, then tap “Open in builder” here and the ready-made search you want (either order works).
Rankings are PvPoke's simulations, not what's winning in the game right now. To include Pokémon that evolve into these, turn on the game's “Show Evolutionary Line” option after pasting. Times are in your time zone.

How the string is put together: in Pokémon GO, & means AND, , means OR, and ! means NOT. Commas are grouped before ampersands, so a&b,c&!d reads as “a AND (b OR c) AND NOT d”.

Terms come from Niantic’s help page plus well-known community terms (e.g. raid, gbl, rocket, gender, specialbackground). The game adds and renames terms now and then, so if one returns nothing it may have changed. Search terms are in English; other game languages use translated words.
